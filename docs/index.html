A patient companion for people who find computers hard

You don't need to know how to use a computer. You just need to know what you want to do.

Tell Naomi what you want to do. She asks what she needs to know, then points at the exact spot on your screen — one gentle step at a time — until it's done.

Like a patient friend sitting beside you

Naomi doesn't teach you how computers work. She helps you do what you came to do.

1

Say what you want

In your own words — typed, or spoken. No technical terms needed.

"How do I make a video call with my son?"
2

Naomi works out what's needed

If something is missing, she asks simply — one question at a time — and helps you find it.

"Do you know his email address? We can find it in an old message."
3

Follow the orange dot

It glides to the exact button on your real screen. Naomi watches, cheers you on, and adapts.

"Good. Now click here."

Built for patience, not for experts

Every detail is designed so nobody ever feels stupid for not knowing something.

🎯

Points at your real screen

Not a video, not a simulation. The dot lands on the actual button, in whatever app you use.

👀

Notices what you do

Right click? "Good!" Something else? "That's okay — let's try this one." Never "wrong".

🧠

Understands the whole goal

She figures out what you'll need — an address, an app, a file — before you get lost.

📝

Remembers for next time

"I'll remember Alysa's email." Kept only on your computer, erasable anytime. Never passwords.

🛡️

Watches out for scams

Gently warns about fake virus popups, gift-card requests, and strangers asking for control.

🗣️

Speaks your language

Reads every step aloud, listens with voice typing, and answers in your language — including বাংলা.

Not another tutorial

Tutorials assume you know the words. Remote-control tools take over. Naomi does neither.

😣 The usual way

  • "Click the hamburger menu, then Settings…"
  • Videos that go too fast
  • A fixed script that breaks when something looks different
  • Someone else takes over your computer

😊 With Naomi

  • "Click here." — and a dot shows exactly where
  • One small step at a time, at your pace
  • Adapts to what's really on your screen
  • You do it yourself — and learn you can
