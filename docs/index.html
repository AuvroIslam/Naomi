Built for patience.

Every detail is designed so no one ever feels foolish for not knowing something.

Points at your real screen

Not a video or a simulation. The dot lands on the actual button, in whatever app you use.

Notices what you do

The right click earns a “Good.” Anything else, a calm “That’s okay — let’s try this one.”

Understands the whole goal

She works out what you’ll need — an address, an app, a file — before you get lost.

Remembers for next time

Kept only on your computer and erasable anytime. Never passwords.

Watches out for scams

A gentle warning about fake virus pop-ups, gift-card requests, and strangers asking for control.

Speaks your language

Reads every step aloud and answers in your language, including বাংলা.

Not another tutorial.

Tutorials assume you know the words. Remote-control tools take over. Naomi does neither.
